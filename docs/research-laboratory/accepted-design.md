# Accepted BTC-Learning design

The intended system is a research collective with four separately formed, source-linked worldviews. It retains initial accounts, then records comparison, revisions, uncertainty and dissent. Research findings and actual financial outcomes remain separately identifiable.

## Accepted starting choices

1. Two separately attributed shared market feeds with explicit agent-specific supplements. Preserve evidence allocation and rotate supplements; compare against equal-input conditions.
2. Begin with BTC, ETH and SOL where supported. Candle/history pairs: one minute / six hours; five minutes / two days; one hour / 28 days; one day / 180 days. Broader multi-coin and multi-scale support remains intended scope. History coverage and runtime costs must be measured.
3. Apply Jev comparison after all four initial accounts are saved. Record exact inputs, question/rubric, returned model version, typed judgment, confidence where supplied, and insufficient-evidence outcomes. Code performs numerical operations; labels do not erase dissent or establish truth.
4. Humans authorize initial orders and transfers. Research agreement or a Jev score does not grant account permissions. A future bounded agent policy requires its own explicit adoption.

## Integrations included in the intended build

Coinbase Prime is the selected financial target. The build includes supported order placement and cancellation, custody, wallet handling and funds transfers, with exact amounts, durable request identities, permission checks, external reconciliation and failure recovery. Real account entitlements, asset/network routes and recovery ownership remain to be specified and verified. A receive address, mock service or research ledger alone does not complete these capabilities.

Looking Glass and a plain inspector must expose the same evidence, claims, revisions and replay positions. Jev is part of the comparison process. The historical simulation environment and its records retain their original meaning.

Approximately ten minutes is a tentative total reasoning-round budget, not a settled prediction horizon. Evidence cutoffs, candle duration, historical window, reasoning time and any future prediction horizon stay distinct.

## Evidence and unresolved questions

The repository provides useful market-data, model-call, versioned-configuration, encrypted-secret and event-chain foundations. The reviewed implementation does not establish the complete new financial subsystem. Research operator access must not become authority to spend; research scores need their actual units, separately from money.

The strongest objection is that correlated weak interpretations can look authoritative in a compelling interface near financial controls. Collective benefit, reader understanding, Jev judgment quality and predictive usefulness remain unproven. Preserve negative results and equal-information, pooled and single-solver comparators.

A proposed fictional paper walkthrough covers missing evidence, an interrupted agent, dissent, a challenged Jev label, partial fill, uncertain submission and revoked authority. It has not been run. It can check design completeness, not prove live integration or research benefit.
