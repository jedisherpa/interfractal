## Q4 Child sequence

What would a child with no context need to see and or hear first, then second, then third, in order to understand what this is for?

### First
- See: Two glowing fiber loops spinning opposite ways around nested shells; a soft Whole/Light door.
- Hear: This is a teaching picture of Hopf loops. Press Enter to go in. Enter is not Yes.

### Second
- See: Small bright points appear on a table while shells keep spinning; a walk of stages toward a hold.
- Hear: People are points. The spinning is weather, not a verdict. Geometry is not permission.

### Third
- See: Three doors together at hold — stay, Commit, Not a fit — and sometimes a Gold act after Commit; funnel arrivals with a named goal.
- Hear: You choose. The picture is evidence. It is not a Yes. Not a fit is a real ending.
