# Joe and Camera

**Status:** approved character lock
**Classification:** key art and in-engine framing
**Date:** 2026-09-17

## Joe lock

Joe is a voxel wizard seen from behind.

| Element | Required look                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Hat     | blue pointed hat with yellow stars and gold brim |
| Beard   | brown                                            |
| Robe    | blue                                             |
| Boots   | brown                                            |
| Wings   | rainbow voxel wings in ROYGBIV order             |
| Staff   | wooden crook staff in the **left hand**          |

Joe is the high-saturation chromatic anchor. He is **not** a combatant.

## Camera lock

- Key art uses chase-cam from behind Joe.
- In-engine Joe may be a billboard, but the approved look still reads from the
  behind-the-shoulder flying frame.
- Keep the world readable around Joe; do not flatten him into UI iconography.

## Framing rules

- Joe should sit in front of the world grammar, not block the landmark.
- The approach frame should let the landmark lead the eye ahead of Joe.
- The interior frame should show Joe inside the grammar of the dimension.
- Joe's rainbow wings are the strongest saturation event in most frames.
