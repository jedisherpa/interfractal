## Material law

- Garden trends ivory stone, pale ground, and soft gold.
- Forest carries dark circuit wood/metal with emissive route glow.
- Desert uses sun-worn monument surfaces, amber emissive cues, and warm ground.
- Core lives in near-black void with blue and red signal energy.
- Cloud Nine is handled separately in
