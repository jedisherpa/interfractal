# Dimension 1 — Forest

**Status:** approved room guide
**Classification:** Scale 1 Circuit Forest
**Date:** 2026-09-17

## Identity

Forest is the Circuit Forest shared room: darker, denser, and route-led rather
than hostile.

## Palette

- clear `#0a2430`
- fog `#123344`
- density `0.0052`
- ambient `#3f9d90`
- emissive `#22d3a6`
- accent `#d946ef`

Cyan marks route. Magenta marks transition.

## Beat A — Approach

Lead with a route landmark ahead of Joe through dark forest volume and luminous
circuit pathways.

## Beat B — Interior

Wrap Joe in the forest grammar itself: layered trunks/forms, signal routes,
transition accents, off-axis well placement, and non-combat civic furniture.

## Notes

- Keep the frame moody, not menacing.
- Do not import drones, patrols, or combat route gates.
