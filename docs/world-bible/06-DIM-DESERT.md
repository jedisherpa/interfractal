# Dimension 2 — Desert

**Status:** approved room guide
**Classification:** Scale 2 Monument Desert
**Date:** 2026-09-17

## Identity

Desert is the Monument Desert shared room: broad air, civic monumentality, and
warm weathered structure.

## Palette

- clear `#38536f`
- fog `#b9ab9c`
- density `0.0045`
- ambient `#4a3a2a`
- emissive `#f59e0b`
- ground `#c27a3a`

## Beat A — Approach

Stage a major monument or plaza landmark ahead of Joe so the destination reads
before the structural details do.

## Beat B — Interior

Once inside, the grammar is warm stone, amber emissive guidance, tetra seating,
and Decision Well offsets rather than fortress or arena language.

## Notes

- Monumentality is public and ceremonial, not military.
- Avoid siege framing, launch-bay silhouettes, or threat lighting.
