# Shot Grammar

**Status:** approved framing guide
**Classification:** reference-frame grammar
**Date:** 2026-09-17

## Two beats per dimension

Every dimension gets exactly two core teaching beats:

| Beat         | Purpose                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------ |
| A — Approach | landmark ahead           |
| B — Interior | world grammar around Joe |

## Approach rule

- Put the landmark ahead of Joe.
- Make destination legible before detail overload.
- Keep Joe present as the color anchor, but not the only subject.

## Interior rule

- Move the camera into the grammar of the world.
- Show how the room behaves around Joe.
- Preserve the off-axis Decision Well and approved civic furniture.

## Cross-dimension consistency

- Chase-cam remains the key art language.
- Joe reads from behind.
- Saturation spikes come from Joe and tightly controlled prism usage.
- Prism color stays between 3% and 12% of the frame.
