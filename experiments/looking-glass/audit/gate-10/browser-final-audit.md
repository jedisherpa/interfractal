# Gate 10 independent closed-browser audit

**Verdict: the prediction workflow is blocked on final candidate `G10-PREDICT-003`.** The browser clicked Start software prediction once. The service accepted a T01 attempt, but the UI discarded its acknowledgement and stayed at `responsePhase:none` with no attached attempt. Answer, Skip and Reveal remained unavailable. No browser draft, commit, skip or reveal occurred; none of the five primary cases or repeat review completed. The exact cause and controlled reproduction are in the [browser-blocker recheck](candidate-003-browser-blocker-recheck.md). Human learning and transfer are untested.

The closed evidence is sound within that negative outcome. I independently recomputed both published SHA-256 fingerprints in **65/65 full browser observations**, including fractional active-tour states. All 65 show the target withheld and no response or revealed result; response-control states match the visible phase. The **16/16 designated paused checkpoints** have exact canonical payloads and fingerprints. Six of those were collected after the **blocked review**, not after a successful reveal. The two events at second 20 join exactly. The tour had 12 active, unpaused observations across the 65 captures, including scroll and resize states. This supports deterministic replay and a natural end, not the missing prediction/reveal lifecycle.
