# Gate 12 — Coordinate without erasing disagreement

Version `gate12-prespec-v1`. Actual user approval is **“Gate 12: Coordinate without erasing disagreement.”** Historical Gates 0–11 and inputs remain immutable. Gate 13 is unapproved. This is a public fictional software instrument with **zero human participants**; it does not execute a human coordination study or the archived shared-meaning protocol.

## Bounded question

Can a declared joint procedure select one simulated action while retaining two incompatible local recommendations, their explicit reference questions and their sources? Can blocked outcomes, a procedural tie-break and a changed-reference control remain inspectable without rewriting earlier judgments or treating a selected action as consensus?

Two fictional groups share one sensor-export slot. Three fixed modes have exact declared values: P1 carries 2 channels in 1 minute; P2 carries 4 in 3 minutes; P3 carries 6 in 5 minutes. Archive's baseline reference ranks more retained channels first; Dispatch's ranks shorter duration first. Their first choices are P3 and P1. Both are correct relative to their own declared reference. No person's actual preference is measured.

The joint procedure minimizes the worse of the two ordinal ranks among currently eligible modes; a tie uses the frozen P1→P2→P3 order. It selects P2 when all modes are eligible. With only P1/P3 eligible it selects P1 by that explicit tie-break. With none eligible it records no joint action. Missing either proposal also blocks selection. The rule is a stipulated procedure, not an inferred shared value, utility optimum, fairness result or endorsement by either group.

There are two reference assignments and three availability scenarios, with four possible proposal-presence states: **24 exact outcomes**. The small sensitivity assignment changes only Dispatch's reference to the existing detail formula in a fresh branch. Both modeled first choices then become P3; normal availability selects P3. Both local reference identities remain explicit, and the earlier branch's P3/P1 judgments remain unchanged. Agreement between two functions is not human consent. Availability filters only the joint procedure; local rankings retain their stated scope over all three fixed modes and are not silently reranked.

## What the instrument must preserve

Every software-recorded local proposal contains its group, exact reference/version, full ranking, preferred mode, source hash and derivation. It is immutable within its branch. A selected joint action cites both exact proposals and the procedure/version, records eligibility, rank comparisons and tie-break, and retains whether its mode matches each original recommendation. A procedural action cannot overwrite either local proposal or claim agreement where the first choices differ. Blocked and repeated-evaluation records remain honest.

The action is an in-memory plan labeled `procedure-selected / not enacted`; it does not send data, reserve equipment, message anyone or carry consent. All acknowledgements and record controls are scripted or source-informed software actions. They must never become evidence of a real group's acceptance, veto, promise or authorization.

Diagram and plain table contain the same source facts, local references, proposal records, procedure inputs/results, histories and actions. Edges mean only declared source/record relationships. No visual advantage, human learning, coordination success or new dimensional claim follows from data preservation. All facts/results are public; there are no hidden answers, participant/session APIs or response/reveal machinery.
