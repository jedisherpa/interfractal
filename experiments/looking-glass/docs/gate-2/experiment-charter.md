## Questions and competing explanations

**Mathematical question:** do the instrument's linked base/fiber selection, common phase, local charts and stereographic representation obey the declared Hopf construction? The intended explanation is exact source algebra plus a finite, clipped representation. Counterexamples include a decorative fiber selected independently of its base, camera motion masquerading as a source change, a chart switch that moves the source point, or a false finite bridge through the projection pole.

**Candidate reference question:** can two transparent questions reorganize the same four supplied fictional records while keeping their identity, differences, unmapped context and alternative views recoverable? The intended explanation is an authored categorical mapping. A plausible negative result is that the geometric layout adds clutter while the equivalent list conveys the facts more clearly. Nothing in this test discovers a natural meaning geometry, group commitment or human-attention mechanism.

**Claims are separate:** numeric tests establish declared model correctness; the chosen observations establish only the stated correspondences/invariances; investigator browser use establishes bounded controls/rendering. Human understanding, learning, coordination improvement and physical extra dimensions remain untested.

## Fixed model and controls

Full equations, chart transitions, source/projection/camera/time distinctions are in `model-contract.md`. Eight fixed S² points identify 128-sample full S³ fibers. **Eight fixed samples plus one custom selected fiber** are displayed. The globe supports picking its visible +Y hemisphere with the exact inverse screen mapping in the model contract; latitude/longitude controls can choose rear points. This does not render the continuum of all fibers. The base sphere is ideal. Rendered fiber selection and a selected base point use the same stable identity.

The primary recorded sequence is 24 seconds, east base, north chart, shared phase 0→2π, fixed projection pole `(0,0,0,1)`, clip radius 4, camera yaw 30°/pitch 20°, stable scale, initial reference `R_CAPACITY_60`, selected record none. Start paused 0. Checkpoints 0/6000/12000/18000/24000ms, step 1000ms. A supported SVG or Canvas renderer may display the computed 3D model; record the actual backend. The exact static cardinal/seam samples, independently derived phase points, expected assignments and tolerances are in `independent-predictions.json`.

Phase and look-around controls create visibly labeled Exploration. Checkpoint/Reset restores the canonical sequence, camera, source selection, chart and reference. Fixed comparisons use the same viewport and scale, targeted at 1280×720 CSS pixels; actual viewport and DPR are recorded. Reduced motion leaves manual step/checkpoint use available. No automatic camera orbit or rescaling may enter a fixed comparison.

The four fictional records and their source hashes are frozen in `fictional-records.json`. Both reference questions, authored mapping rules, exact assignment rows/reasons and arbitrary phases are frozen in `reference-mappings.json`. Weather remains intentionally unmapped. No factual update or source-record edit occurs during reference switching.

## Decision, revisions, and stop boundary
