# Gate 5 experiment charter

Version `gate-5-charter-v1`. Approved scope: **Two additional coordinates in 5D**. This is a prespecification, not a result. The question is whether the instrument exposes two independent extra-coordinate controls, which observations recover those coordinates, and which ambiguities remain. Mathematical fidelity, conditional recovery, instrumental comparison and human understanding are separate outcomes. No human study or Gate 6 work is authorized.

An agent inspecting privileged coordinates checks an instrument, not blinded comprehension. Fixtures cannot replace actual controls or inspected images.

## Captures and bounded execution

Freeze all contracts/predictions and the baseline pin before browser execution. Build immutable candidate001 and independently audit model/build/routes. Root then conducts actual supported computer-use trials; fixture scripts do not perform or manufacture browser actions.

Preserve originals for comparison at0/0,45/0,45/45 under equal viewport/framing; slice point/empty under equal framing; active first, second and return legs where actually captured; restored state and any decisive failure. The packet selects two–four useful originals and links the others. Image existence is not inspection. Record clipping, overlapping geometry, misleading labels and hidden controls separately from numeric success. Video is claimed only if actually recorded.

An essential model/replay/control failure stops that candidate. Preserve its exact source/build/run/log/images before the smallest justified repair. Allow at most two revisions after the initial candidate; repeat affected checks and a relevant counterexample. Do not broaden the hypothesis or change scoring to seek a positive result. Qualified, mixed, negative and inconclusive results are valid. Report concrete blockers if a watchable replay or required observation cannot be obtained.

## Claims and stop

Success supports the declared source/slice equations, isolated controls, conditional rank-five recovery and recorded instrument comparison. It does not establish learning, perceived usability, optimal navigation, physical extra dimensions, arbitrary source uniqueness, or full 32-point separation at45/45. Equal radii remain ambiguous without known plane values; β=0 observations leave v hidden.
