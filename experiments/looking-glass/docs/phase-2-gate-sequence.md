# Looking Glass continuation: proposed Gates 8–15

Version `phase2-sequence-v1`. Paul's instruction, “Orchestrate this, in the same way as this whole experiment,” authorizes defining this continuation and executing **Gate 8 only**, followed by the same human review boundary as Gates 0–7. The old gate-sequence document remains a historical artifact. Gates 9–15 below are proposals, not current work or evidence of approval.

The continuation asks what observations can establish, then whether people can use them. Its first result must be a bounded software benchmark. Later claims about choice, learning, disagreement, persuasion or ordinary use require actual people and actual observations. Synthetic records, agents and scripted interface checks cannot substitute for them. No recruiting, messages to prospective participants, external study, public deployment or execution of the archived shared-meaning protocol is authorized here.

| **9 — Choose, watch, or inspect a static sheet (proposed)** | Compare selecting observations with watching the identical ordered observations and inspecting the identical static observation set. | **11 — Break a summary by changing its receiver (proposed)** | A small fictional-record family whose outward summaries agree on one receiver task but disagree on another; inspect missing dependencies and a minimal repair. | Counterexample worlds, declared receiver functions, provenance-preserving repair and equivalent plain representation. | **13 — A persuasive but wrong view (proposed)** | A frozen counterexample with a polished misleading representation, available checking evidence, and a disclosed correction/debrief plan. | Independently verified mismatch and equal evidence access. | **14 — Geometry ablations (proposed)** | Compare the smallest useful geometric view with cards, a table and a plain graph using identical facts, queries and tasks. Preserve negative outcomes; a simpler view may suffice. Avoid multiplying conditions beyond what an approved study can support. | Decide Gate 15. |
| **15 — Retention and ordinary use (proposed)** | A defined delayed retest and a limited ordinary-use interval on a concrete task; log abandonment, errors and practical burden as well as success. | Actual elapsed time and actual users/use, with consent, retention window, attrition and missingness prespecified. Simulation cannot establish retention or adoption. | Bounded synthesis and separate future scope only. |
