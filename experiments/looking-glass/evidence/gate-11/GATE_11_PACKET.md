For a selected field set D, global sufficiency means that **every pair of worlds with identical complete receiver payloads has the same receiver answer**. Reusing the old count answer as a release decision produces three false positives: W00, W01 and W10. This deliberately wrong shortcut is labeled separately and never used by the sound evaluator.

For the count receiver, no additions are needed: its minimum is zero. For release, every singleton fails globally; removing calibration from the successful pair leaves W01/W11 as a counterexample, and removing authorization leaves W10/W11. These certificates prove minimality only over this declared finite family and menu. They do not establish shortest encoding, universal completeness or necessity for every individual payload. A precomputed receiver decision would be a different, excluded menu entry.

Local and global results are deliberately separate. One supplied negative dependency determines local **no** under the AND rule even if the other dependency is absent. A supplied positive dependency alone remains locally insufficient. Thus calibration-only W00 can be determined while the field subset still fails for W10/W11. “Missing” does not automatically mean every local answer is unknown.

## Receiver boundary and evidence scope

The receiver is a pure function of its declared payload and receiver ID. It has no world lookup, source resolver or access to the inspection envelope. Logical source pointers inside each payload are intentionally identical across the alternative worlds; unique world IDs, source hashes, revision IDs and actual source paths are retained outside that input. Opening the full-source panel is public inspection, not covert enrichment of the summary. Repairs explicitly copy fields into the payload and record their origin. All worlds and answers are public; this is input isolation, not secrecy.

The independent candidate audit checked 32 projected payloads across both receivers, malformed/covert inputs, immutable bytes, certificates, checkpoints and read-only routes: **190 checks**, plus the publication-race recheck. The final closed-core verifier passed **2,567 checks**, including historical files and evidence structure. These are software checks, not a sample of successful users. See the [candidate audit](../../audit/gate-11/candidate-002-audit.md), [final audit](../../audit/gate-11/final-audit.md) and [reproducible final verification](../../audit/gate-11/final-core-verification.json).

The accepted complete acquisition contains **100 actual DOM observations, 79 host actions and six untouched JPEG originals**. All 100 semantic, information and receiver-payload fingerprints independently recompute, and all 100 published viewport/scene measurements match the actual DOM, including post-export layouts. There is no recorded horizontal overflow at the inspected 1280- and 960-pixel widths. Lower source, provenance, history and export panels require scrolling. The witness panel also requires internal scrolling to read all facts. Numerical identity and these images do not establish equal effort or human readability.

All 18 designated paused comparisons match: seven baseline checkpoints **after the critical first-use path**, seven after further local exploration, two after Reopen saved and two after reload. “Baseline” is a comparison pass, not a pre-exposure human condition. The actual uninterrupted tour lasts 24.005 seconds and matches all seven scheduled events, including the final restore→automatic stop chain at second 24. Multiple active snapshots are retained. The 12-second partial state is recorded in an actual scheduled event and paused checkpoint, but no separate active DOM observation was captured during seconds 12–16. Manual checkpoint navigation is distinct from natural completion.

Two document exports contain truthful prefixes of 56 and 21 events, each including its own export action and an included-through sequence. Later clicks remain outside those prefixes and are retained in the host trace. The first document holds seven real local repair creations; after reload the second has no local history. Canonical parents remain references, not fabricated local actions. The final observation is paused at zero. No OS reduced-motion setting was emulated.
