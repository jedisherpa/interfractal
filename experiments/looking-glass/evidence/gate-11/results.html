The smallest repair in this menu

The menu allows calibration, authorization and paint color. All eight subsets were checked. Neither no repair nor paint alone helps. Calibration alone leaves W10/W11 unresolved; authorization alone leaves W01/W11 unresolved. Both dependencies suffice. Adding paint as well is redundant.

Calibration plus authorization is the unique globally minimal repair, with two menu entries. This minimum applies only to these four worlds and this menu. It is not a universal minimum, shortest encoding or claim that every individual decision needs both fields.
