Source basis and limits

The circle-fiber and stereographic-line interpretation follows Niles Johnson and David W. Lyons. Ralph Cohen’s notes support local trivializations and the section/triviality boundary. The equations use an explicit complex-coordinate/sign convention; raw components from other conventions require conversion.

S³ has three intrinsic dimensions in R⁴. This is not yet a rotating solid 4D object. The mathematical sources do not prove a psychology of attention, collective meaning, physical extra dimensions, or the chosen record semantics. Four source records are extracted from supplied fictional repair-event facts; no participant study or original four-agent language experiment was run.
