The 2D exact-site excess and `<8 SVG units` marker-pair proxy are screen-space measurements; neither is a physical occlusion or usability score.

## Qualifications

At 1280×720 the playback buttons touch the viewport bottom and seek/checkpoint controls require vertical scrolling; the exact ID/status paragraph is small and dense. At 960×720 the main angle controls and panels remain visible but lower controls still require scrolling. The detailed roster is available through inspect actions and the inspector, so visual recognition of every vertex from the wireframe alone is not established. The inspector itself is below the main views. Two historical Gate 3/Gate 4 links opened the correct paused run/build through actual UI and DOM, but the supported native new-tab screenshot call failed once for each; those errors are retained in `historical-ui-review.json` and no historical image is claimed. Paused/manual controls were tested; no OS reduced-motion preference was emulated.

These checks establish the declared mathematical and interface behavior, conditional recovery from known raw-coordinate observations and a route-specific structural/action comparison. They do not establish reconstruction from arbitrary images or unknown transformations, uniquely 5D physical causation, human comprehension, relative usability or an optimal navigation cost. The equal-radius slice pairs differ in their known plane coordinates even though their 3D slices match. No Gate 6 work, study, deployment or external message belongs to this audit.
