## Central question

Under what conditions do interacting participants develop a sustained collective capability, how is that capability stewarded, and how can it participate in larger patterns while its internal relationships remain visible and revisable?

## Paul’s latest corrections govern the research

1. The eight aspects of self and twelve prescribed lenses were provisional design choices. Neither is a discovered universal. Any change needs an explicit reason. Group size may be any number; 3, 4, 5 are useful small-model comparisons, with 12 a larger comparison, not a ceiling.
2. Convergence means distinct parts develop a usable pattern/capability while differences persist. It need not be unanimity, synchronized opinions, or averaged outputs.
3. A person becomes sufficiently coherent for a relationship; a group can develop a capability exceeding what one participant could do alone; that group can participate as a unit in a larger arrangement. Feedback from the larger arrangement changes internal coordination. This is a hypothesis/program, not an established universal ladder. Individuals can develop coherence through participation.
4. Affinity, functional coordination, emergence of capability, stewardship, influence, leadership, and who can commit whom all matter. Do not turn all this into only a consent audit or only topology.
5. Shared reference, observed influence, and formally recognized authority must be distinguishable. A geometric center need not be a leader. A leader is not automatically illegitimate. Study arrangements rather than assuming flatness or hierarchy is best.
6. The pictures, guides, games, and existing code are “crayon drawings trying to explain it.” Preserve explanatory ambition; do not dismiss them as mere decoration or treat their details as natural laws.
7. Point / network or polyhedron / loop / sphere / torus are candidate representations of different aspects or resolutions. Organizational scale, spatial dimension, degrees of freedom, time, and visual resolution are distinct.
8. Prescribed lenses (e.g. Logician) and self-authored contributions are distinct. A person may take multiple lenses; none defines a whole person. Investigate representational and framing effects, including analogy and recategorization from Hofstadter and Sander’s Surfaces and Essences.
9. The visualizer should let each participant express how they see the situation and inspect differences. A collective can have a shared outward expression while retaining recoverable internal differences and history.
10. Humans attest; agents do not ratify. Current session corrections outrank historical drafts. AI-generated review is not external expert endorsement.
