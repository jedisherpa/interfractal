## Critical path

Source/construct freeze → minimal evidence instrument → semantic bench → cognitive interviews and measurement work. Then two branches can progress: (A) visualizer sample planning and registered B/C comparison; (B) capability, interaction and collective-interface studies with their own feasibility and sample plan. The second can use the plain workspace and does not depend on positive graphics results. Synthetic compression cases can start earlier.

Literature analysis, event-fixture design and task development can proceed together after the shared brief. Confirmatory sample planning depends on actual endpoints and plausible variance, not on a preferred participant count. Cross-scale work depends on a usable group interface and credible within-group measures. Optional 3D does not block the initial plain/2D comparison.

## If the deadline arrives first

**Before instrument readiness:** deliver the research charter, exact-source audit, synthetic traces, limitations and an instrument demonstration labeled as a prototype. Claim that the research is specified, not validated.

**After P1 but before human evidence:** demonstrate source recovery, changed constraints, corrections and same-picture/different-meaning counterexamples. Report the actual bounded bench results. Make no claim about user comprehension or collective advantage.

**After P2:** report feasibility, observed misunderstandings and the revised design with denominators and uncertainty. Do not turn a small pilot into a success verdict.

**After a powered, registered P3:** report the prespecified effect and uncertainty for the tested population/tasks/versions. A positive B/C result supports practical visualizer value in that setting; interaction advantage and universal multiscale emergence still require their own tests.

## Work ownership

The human research/study owner is accountable for purpose, deadline, budget, recruitment setting, review requirements and claims made externally. No individual has been assigned that role by this package. A methods owner defines estimands, scoring and sample planning; a cognition/HCI owner checks interpretation; an engineering owner implements the record/view contract; a coordination owner defines authority and stewardship cases; a verifier challenges the actual instrument. One person can hold multiple roles, but outcome scoring and consequential acceptance checks should have a second reader.

AI sessions provide documented analytical assistance. Their independent first passes reduce conversational anchoring; they do not constitute independent laboratories, licensed expertise or real-world expert endorsement. External subject-matter review remains valuable where claims exceed the tested cases.

## Decisions that can be made now

- Keep 4 as the first fixture size because it matches existing prototype assumptions and a tractable role allocation. Preserve arbitrary roster support. Treat 3/5 as nearby checks and 12 as a larger stress case, not a natural ceiling.
- Keep eight aspects and twelve lenses as candidate vocabularies; initial contributions are self-authored. Record reasons before changing a vocabulary or experimental count.
- Build the evidence contract and usable plain baseline first; add coordinated 2D views against the same data. Keep live LLMs and optional 3D out of the first causal comparison.
- Separate task capability, representation accuracy, authority and private participation reports. No universal coherence score.
- Preserve dissent and uncertainty in group summaries. A group's outward interface needs mandate, limits and dependencies as well as a compact capability claim.

## Inputs needed before human enrollment, not before development

Responsible owner and setting; actual deadline; recruitment feasibility and compensation; required review/consent; accessibility and privacy arrangements; instrument/task versions and scoring; explicit missingness/stopping rules. Before P3 additionally choose worthwhile benefit δ, tolerable harm h, confidence/error rules and a defensible number of independent teams. These are substantive design decisions, not fields to fill with convenient default numbers.
