# Collective Capability Across Scales

Research launch brief for Paul Cooper · 20 September 2026 · Design v0.3

**The research question:** Under what conditions, if any, can an interactive visual model help participants build, test, and revise shared meaning across perspectives and scales, improving coordination while preserving consequential differences and participant agency?

The original question about sustained collective capability, stewardship, and participation in larger arrangements remains the broader program. The current focus tests shared meaning as a candidate mechanism and evaluates the visualizer separately.

The v0.2 baseline was specified, independently challenged and revised. This v0.3 framing and proposed language bench have not received a new independent review. Five separate specialist analyses were followed by an independent adversarial review, two targeted cross-checks and verification of the resulting corrections. These were independent AI sessions with defined remits, not a panel of human experts or independent laboratories.

## What we are preserving

Your central idea is that differentiated contributions can become a functional whole, and that whole can participate in another arrangement without losing its internal life. Convergence means a usable coordination pattern; it does not require everyone to think alike. Shared reference, affinity, influence, authority and stewardship remain distinct.

The drawings provide candidate ways to see these relationships. A group can be a point in one view and an inspectable network in another. A trajectory can show changing participation. The research must establish what each view preserves and whether it improves a judgment or action. It assumes no universal progression from polygon to sphere or torus.

Eight aspects and twelve lenses remain candidate vocabularies. Four participants is the first modeling choice because it is tractable and matches existing prototype assumptions. Three and five are nearby comparisons; twelve is a larger stress case, not a ceiling. Changes require a recorded reason.

## The staged program

| Stage | Concrete result | Requirement to advance |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Framing and source audit | Charter, definitions, independent analyses, implementation findings | Completed and challenged in this package |
| 2. Evidence instrument | Versioned records, capable plain workspace, relationship/timeline views, outward group interface | Correct sources, meanings, corrections and permissions across a bounded counterexample corpus |
| 3. Interpretation and feasibility | Adult cognitive interviews, usable tasks, reliable logs and scoring | Prespecified feasibility criteria; no efficacy claim from the pilot |
| 4A. Visualizer value | Randomized team comparison: plain workspace versus the same workspace with visual views | Frozen task bank, worthwhile benefit/harm bounds, defensible sample and registered analysis |
| 4B. Capability and scale | Interaction comparisons, useful group summaries, external dependencies and revision | Its own measures and study design; can proceed using the plain workspace |
| 5. Durability | Repeated participation, changing requirements, substitution, turnover and repair | Evidence over a declared period, with cross-group dependence handled |

Paths 4A and 4B are independent research questions. **A negative visualizer result does not stop the capability research.** Synthetic interface models can develop earlier; human cross-group studies need their own feasibility and inference plan.

## How to use the visualizer effectively

Build around one inspectable evidence record. Provide a readable plain view, typed relationships, a history of change and a compact outward offer with dependencies, limits and mandate. Add geometry where it helps answer a specified question. Preserve the ability to recover an internal disagreement or correct a misleading summary.

The source audit found three consequential issues to address before research use: the inspected LensForge synthesis path uses round-one responses while the feed includes round two; generated endorsers are model attributions rather than human attestations; and some abacus feeling/conflict graphics derive from synthetic hashes and chosen display rules. These are pinned static-source findings, not demonstrated production incidents. Exact source snapshots and links are included.

The first comparison holds process, evidence and operational capabilities constant while offering the visualizer in one condition. It estimates the practical effect of that offered package, including navigation and adoption costs. Later studies can isolate geometric encoding, lens scaffolding, teaching methods and live AI.

## What has been checked

The delivered Python benchmark passes 32 specified lantern plan-state combinations, separate physical/authorization/readiness judgments for four proposals, and four exclusive-resource request cases. One counterexample shows that the same summary can hide different readiness; another shows that identical two-kit offers can hide a shared battery that prevents simultaneous delivery. Reviewers rechecked the keys. These are synthetic demonstrations, not human findings or application tests.

The package also integrates the accessible ideas around *Surfaces and Essences*: analogy as recognition and recategorization, surfaces as potentially useful cues, and the need to test transfer to unfamiliar situations. The complete book was unavailable; the analysis uses accessible author material and related primary research and discloses that limit.
