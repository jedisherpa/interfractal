# Current game rules

Each Self begins with four outward allocations and four inward support faces. A Lens is authored Vision, Territory and Duties, with perspective terms and resource references. The four demo perspective terms are seed examples, not permanent offices attached to geometric edges. A Lens may be moved among goals; a person may hold multiple Lenses. One Lens cannot occupy several edges at once.

A Shared Goal has four edges. The current game hypothesis requires four distinct participants, four active edges and coverage of that goal's needed perspectives for coherence. Duplicate perspectives or empty staffing do not produce a coherent team. Activation additionally requires evidenced progress and activated prerequisites. Geometric closure communicates coherence, not completion.

Attention Prism focus is independent of contribution. Effort is an independent bounded commitment budget. A larger allocation capacity does not reduce effort. Overextension creates a visible Expansion Threshold; unsupported work cannot be claimed completed.

Commands preserve proposals, owner acceptance, Advice, responsible decisions, outcomes and correction history as distinct records. Potential Material Impact uses explicit affected Lenses, expertise and structural overlap; it is an explainable demo inference, not natural-language constitutional adjudication. Consultation obligations stay attached to affected work through transfers and corrections. Unrelated work can proceed. Governance is a human-directed request with reasoning, not a automatic enactment.

PLAY gathers proposals and allocations. All active people record readiness before round close. COORDINATE records relevant Advice and separate decisions; Lens/work adjustments can repair issues. ACT records completed or missed work with evidence. RETURN applies each new outcome once, recomputes progress and dependencies, and opens the next PLAY. Uncommunicated overdue work stays visible and affects explainable eligibility.

The configured Tribe hypothesis allows a reliable eligible player to propose one lower-face support agreement with three other people. Every member, including the beneficiary, accepts. One fully active agreement adds `tribeMultiplier` allocations (default 4), never effort credits. Lost support removes its dependent capacity in any phase, retains endangered allocations/work and creates a Threshold. Repair requires actual restored support, withdrawal, or accepted transfer. Repeated restoration retains lineage and cannot duplicate capacity. Circular support is rejected. None of this assigns differentiated constitutional supporter offices or removes human membership.

Group actions derive their actor from private seat credentials. The creator cannot act for another seat. Private relational details are owner-scoped at the service boundary. Practice/sandbox actors are explicitly simulated; game evidence remains a participant attestation, not independently verified physical reality.
