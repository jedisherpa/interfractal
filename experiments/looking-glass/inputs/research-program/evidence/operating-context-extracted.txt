3. The two standing questions
The portfolio still answers two questions. Revenue work must serve them, not replace them.
How can different people organize around a shared light without one person swallowing the others?
How can many forms of intelligence explore, specialize, build, and learn without quietly turning ability into power?
The desired system is not a crown for machines and not an invisible crown for a founder. It is a governed world in which intelligence can grow, roles can emerge, useful artifacts can persist, mistakes can become inherited evidence, and consequential authority remains explicit, attributable, limited, challengeable, and human.
