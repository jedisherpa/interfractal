# How the original idea becomes a researchable model

## The phenomenon we are trying to explain

Distinct people become able to do something together. Their arrangement acquires enough reliability that others can interact with it as a useful unit. The arrangement continues to depend on internal differences, responsibilities and relationships, even when an outside observer sees one offer or service. External demands can then reorganize those internal relationships. The research concerns the formation, recognition, maintenance and revision of that capability, including how people direct it and remain able to contest its representation.

This preserves the user's center, movement and changing-scale intuitions. A shared center can be a reference that makes differentiated action intelligible. Motion can depict changing relationships over time. A group can appear as a point in a wider diagram while retaining a rich internal history. The rigorous step is to specify which relation each representation preserves, which it omits, and what prediction that choice helps someone make.

## Five connected research problems

| Problem | Candidate account | Observation that would challenge it |
|---|---|---|
| Formation | Complementary information/resources plus interaction allow coordinated action | Matched independent pooling or one informed coordinator performs as well at lower cost |
| Recognition | People learn to notice a useful relational pattern across changing appearances | They recognize the familiar picture but fail on a new story with the same dependency |
| Maintenance | Memory, correction, substitution and stewardship sustain a task capability through changes | Performance collapses after a small perturbation or the departure of one expert |
| Representation | A compact, revisable interface makes the collective usable from another scale | Hidden dependencies repeatedly invalidate its outward promise |
| Visualization | Inspectable visual views improve some of these judgments/actions | The equally usable plain workspace matches or outperforms them, or graphics increase false certainty |

These problems are related but not interchangeable. A positive result on one does not supply the missing evidence for the others. Group capability need not be a novel causal mechanism; the explanation may be a familiar but well-arranged combination of resources and coordination.

## What *Surfaces and Essences* contributes

The complete book was unavailable. This synthesis draws on the publisher's description, Sander's complete author-uploaded interview, related original cognitive research and the independent cognition memo. It does not claim a chapter-by-chapter reading.

The authors' account treats analogy and categorization as central to recognizing a present situation through prior experience. For this project, the useful question is how people come to see scattered contributions as an intelligible capability, and how that interpretation changes. A category is not merely a fixed slot into which an already understood person is placed. Surfaces can provide the cues through which useful relationships first become accessible. Their value is tested by what the learner can subsequently recognize and do. [Sander interview](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/260288848_The_central_role_of_analogy_in_cognitive_science); [book description](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/douglas-r-hofstadter/surfaces-and-essences/9780465018475/).

That gives the crayon drawings a specific research role: they are proposed aids to noticing and reorganizing relationships. A torus may make recurrence memorable; a point with an expandable interior may make scale-dependent description intuitive. We should test whether those cues help people recognize the relation after the familiar drawing is removed, and whether they can reject a similar drawing when the relation is absent. This is an application proposed by this program, not a result established by the book.

Chalmers, French and Hofstadter also warn that providing an analogy system with carefully preselected representations can hide the difficult work of forming those representations. For LensForge, this creates a concrete design challenge: twelve prescribed lenses might help participants notice neglected considerations, but they might also manufacture the apparent pattern. Record the original contribution, participant's grouping, system suggestion and later revision separately. Permit overlapping, alternative and unmapped contributions. [Primary technical report](https://consc.net/papers/highlevel.pdf), inspected via supplied extraction; direct web opening failed.

Gentner, Loewenstein and Thompson's experiments provide narrower empirical support for comparing examples as a way to improve learning and transfer in specified negotiation tasks. They do not validate a universal theory of collective emergence. Their relevance is a testable teaching intervention: compare two situations side by side, identify the relation, then test an unfamiliar case and an opportunity to act on it. Run this separately from a rendering comparison so its effect is identifiable. [Original study](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/GentnerLoewensteinThompson03.pdf).

The supplied guides add a practical distinction that the interface can make observable: exploring a possibility, undertaking a commitment and revisiting it are different events. A proposal can be understood without being accepted; a commitment can be revised without erasing the earlier dependency it created. These guide openings establish intended practice, not evidence that the practice works. The cognitive program extends beyond agreement: it asks whether people discover the right dependency, anticipate the collective's limits and revise their account when consequences disagree with it.

## A mathematical starting point

Let \(X_g(t)\) be a group's recorded task-relevant state: contributions, resources, dependencies, current actions, recognized authority and relevant history. Let \(u(t)\) be an outside request or perturbation, and \(q(t)\) be an independently scored task outcome. A provisional dynamics model is

\[
X_g(t+1)\sim P_\theta(\cdot\mid X_g(t),u(t)).
\]

This is a candidate modeling family. It does not assume that records capture the whole person, that the process is actually Markovian, or that social states are quantum states. Add history or change the model when prediction demands it.

The outward interface is \(Y_g=\pi_g(X_g)\). Its adequacy depends on what another group needs to predict or coordinate. If two internal states produce the same outward summary but react differently to a relevant request, the summary has omitted something consequential. Enrich it with a dependency, limit or uncertainty marker and test again. Compare prediction error and coordination cost against full information and a naive summary. This follows the problem-dependent spirit of state-space compression, without claiming that a particular grouping is naturally privileged. [Wolpert et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.7403).

The exact closure condition and Hopf construction are in `reviews/R1_MATHEMATICS.md`. An empirical approximation must declare its task range, errors and input conditions. No chosen number of participants or shape establishes closure. Because compression is many-to-one, retain a separate source record for recovering relevant internal differences.

Dynamics can be functional without everyone moving alike. Alternating handoffs, distributed expertise and revisable roles may sustain output while beliefs remain different. Measure performance residence time, recovery after changes, dependency failures and substitution. Synchronization, visual symmetry and perceived unity can be measured separately when meaningful; none is a substitute for those outcomes.

## Stewardship and larger scales

Leadership can include setting direction, attracting uptake, coordinating work, exercising a delegated decision right and representing the group outwardly. Those functions can coincide or be distributed. A central point cannot tell us which is happening. Record the relevant evidence and scope.

A group becomes a useful participant at another scale when its outward expression lets others make warranted decisions: what it can provide, under which limits, through whose mandate, with which dependencies, and how updates will return. Test whether it can receive a new constraint, locate the affected internal work and revise its external promise. This operational meaning of scale is available without settling collective consciousness or assuming a universal developmental ladder.

The next build therefore needs four complementary things: a readable evidence record, inspectable relationships, a history of changes, and a compact outward interface. Geometry earns its place by improving a defined inference or action. The program allows it to be a strong teaching tool, a useful operational view, both, or neither, depending on the evidence.
