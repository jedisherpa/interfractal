# R2 independent adversarial review

Date: 19 September 2026. Reviewer: a separate AI analysis session, not an external human expert. Scope: the six integrated program documents, first-session materials, lantern JSON, reference scorer and saved result; the R1 mathematics and experimental-design memos were consulted. No human study, repository runtime test or new literature review was performed. This review preserves the frozen R1 files.

## Disposition

**The package can be finalized after two bounded design corrections, with the later-stage prerequisites remaining explicit.** Correct the dependency graph so research on collective interfaces does not require a positive geometric-interface trial, and distinguish an intentionally presented fictional error from a consequential attribution defect. The program already avoids most major overclaims: it separates task capability from agreement and authority, preserves provisional counts, limits source findings to pinned code, and does not call its synthetic check human validation.

The principal remaining danger is research drift. A meticulous permission and interface study could finish while the owner's question—how a differentiated group sustains a capability and becomes a useful participant at another scale—remains largely untouched. The design should keep that question on an independently advancing track.

## Numbered findings

### 1. High — an unnecessary dependency puts the central research question behind the visualizer trial

**Location:** `02_AGENT_PERSPECTIVES_AND_RUNBOOK.md`, dependency graph (`Powered comparative study` → `Cross-scale transfer and field replication`); `05_EXECUTION_PLAN_AND_GATES.md`, linear critical path; compare `03_INTEGRATED_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`, P0's five distinct claims and P5.

The charter correctly allows a useful plain workspace and separates representation advantage from compression adequacy. The orchestration graph nevertheless implies that cross-scale work follows the powered visualizer comparison. This could make an expensive or negative visualizer trial delay research on collective capability, although a plain interface could support that research. The caveat that P4/P5 are not automatic rewards does not state the affirmative alternative path.

**Remediation:** branch after a faithful instrument and suitable measurements exist. One branch investigates B/C practical value. Another develops and tests task-relevant collective interfaces, resource-matched interaction comparisons and cross-group dependencies, using a working plain interface if necessary. A particular *human multigroup experiment* still requires its own feasibility, sample and interference planning; it does not require evidence that graphics improve four-person task scores. Synthetic compression tests and task development can begin earlier. Preserve longer-term field work as later work, without making all multiscale inquiry wait for it.

**Disposition:** fix before finalizing the package, because the current operational graph can contradict the declared aim. This does not require building the second instrument now.

### 2. Medium — the deliberate false-attribution probe conflicts with the stop rule

**Location:** `studies/FIRST_SESSION_MATERIALS.md`, facilitator step 6; `03_INTEGRATED_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`, Human-study readiness and records; `04_VISUALIZER_RESEARCH_SPEC.md`, acceptance corpus.

Step 6 intentionally presents “D approved the bridge plan before S4.” The general stop rule calls for stopping an affected session for false attributed commitments. Read literally, the intended probe triggers a stop. More seriously, an implementer could insert that false claim into the canonical event record to create the stimulus, thereby breaking the provenance contract the study is evaluating.

**Remediation:** identify the claim as a planned fictional task stimulus, shown in a separate suggested-interpretation panel or probe card. Consent/instructions may disclose that examples can contain mistakes without revealing the answer. The underlying source record must remain accurate. Distinguish the fictional characters' stipulated decisions from live participants' actual assent. An unscripted system attribution to a real participant, a corrupted canonical record, or any created action right remains a consequential defect and follows the stop/repair rule. Do not conceal a real defect by relabeling it an experiment.

**Disposition:** clarify before finalization and before implementing the probe. No human result is necessary to settle this design inconsistency.

### 3. Medium — the delivered counterexample chiefly establishes permission loss, not capability compression

**Location:** `studies/lantern_task.json`, S3/S4; `studies/verify_fixture.py`, compression counterexample; protocol P1 and P5; charter's formation and maintenance questions.

The S3/S4 pair is valid: identical route/count summaries conceal a relevant endorsement-version change. Its physical feasibility is identical in both states. Therefore it establishes that the summary omits a permission-relevant distinction. It does not yet exercise two internally different groups with the same outward capability offer and different ability to deliver under an external request.

The documentation correctly limits the fixture, so this is not a false result. It is a coverage gap that matters because the available demonstration otherwise disproportionately teaches attestation rather than emergent capability. Three role cards contain physical constraints while the fourth primarily contains a permission rule; four participants are not thereby shown to be necessary for the physical task.

**Remediation:** retain this fixture as an authority/version calibration. Add an early, explicitly separate capability case: two groups export the same capacity but one depends on a shared unavailable specialist or mutually exclusive resource; introduce an external request that separates their achievable outputs. The augmented summary should resolve the difference and state its scope. Also include a case where a competent individual or coordinator can solve the task efficiently, so the task bank does not stipulate the desired group advantage. Neither case proves human emergence.

**Disposition:** an acknowledged P1/task-development prerequisite, not a reason to withhold the current design package. A source-grounded capability counterexample would strengthen the immediate deliverable if time permits.

### 4. Medium — task selection can manufacture apparent visualizer value even with sound randomization

**Location:** protocol P2, task difficulty checks; P3 Endpoint and scoring; P4 resource-matched comparison.

Randomization can identify the B/C effect for the selected battery. It does not establish that the battery represents the user's intended work. Developing tasks alongside the visualizer can preferentially retain puzzles whose structure happens to fit its views. A well-randomized result would then establish task-specific interface advantage while encouraging a much broader claim about collective capability. The current bounds on generalization help, but the task-selection process remains unspecified.

**Remediation:** define the intended task domain and dimensions before selecting confirmatory forms: complementary information, complementary resources or skills, revision, uncertainty, coordination cost, and outward dependency. Document why tasks enter or leave the bank. Obtain a display-agnostic check of constraints and difficulty; retain novel held-out forms that were not used for interface tuning. Include tasks where direct coordination should suffice as boundary cases. A compact preregistered table of task coverage is enough initially; a large universal battery is unnecessary.

**Disposition:** required before P3 registration, explicitly deferrable in the present package. Do not call the current lantern fixture a validated capability instrument or representative task sample.

### 5. Medium — the confirmatory decision rule needs an explicit inconclusive region

**Location:** protocol P3, Sample planning and decision rules; P2 provisional feasibility percentages.

The proposed lower-confidence-bound-above-δ rule is deliberately demanding and can be defensible. The documentation correctly requires planning around effects above δ and does not treat the small pilot as powered efficacy evidence. However, an interval entirely above zero but straddling δ is also inconclusive for the worthwhile-benefit decision; the prose highlights only intervals spanning benefit and harm. Likewise, a favorable primary interval with uncertain participation harm cannot be called an overall advance.

**Remediation:** write a complete decision table before registration: all benefit/harm criteria met; worthwhile benefit excluded; unacceptable harm indicated; otherwise inconclusive. Separate lack of evidence for benefit from evidence of unacceptable harm. Keep δ, h, interval level, multiplicity policy, missing-data sensitivity and harm denominators unresolved until genuine decision owners select them. No convenient numerical defaults are needed now.

**Disposition:** clarify the general residual category now if convenient; numerical completion and sample calculation remain P3 prerequisites. The 90% and 80% pilot targets are transparently provisional workflow criteria, not scientific constants or assurances.

### 6. Low — model fit and data semantics should remain distinct as the specification grows

**Location:** visualizer Geometry contract and Canonical data contract; protocol P1; R1 mathematics, coarse-state closure.

Stable IDs, source links and replay establish what a display is based on. They do not show that a chosen distance, edge, center or summary predicts collective behavior. The package currently makes this distinction well. Future engineering acceptance could nevertheless drift toward treating complete provenance as construct validity, especially when a synthetic hash has a clear technical derivation.

**Remediation:** retain three separate acceptance labels in implementation records: computational correctness, adequate task semantics, and human/usefulness evidence. A documented hash can pass the first and fail the latter two. For an approximate collective interface, specify held-out external inputs, prediction target, loss and tolerated failures before calling compression adequate. A labeled torus analogy may remain a teaching view without passing a social-model test.

**Disposition:** a continuing control, not a present blocker. No general geometry-to-psychology inference was found in the integrated specification.

## Strongest counterexample

A knowledgeable coordinator using the plain workspace rapidly combines the constraints, retains differences, uses an appropriate delegated mandate, and issues a compact outward offer that other groups use successfully. The visualizer adds navigation cost. Teams still possess collective capability, and their outward interface still works, but geometry offers no incremental value and measured interaction gain over this competent alternative may be absent.

This outcome should lead to a useful plain-interface research/product path, further inquiry into the conditions under which coordination adds value, and a narrower role for geometry. It must not be reclassified as failure to reach the “right” developmental shape. The charter allows this result; finding 1 makes the execution path allow it too.

## Verification and claim limits

I inspected the fixture functions and manually checked the constraints, then recomputed all 32 plan/state evaluations using the reference functions. The declared physical plans and readiness states agree: S1 has two physical plans and permits v1; S2 has only the bridge physical plan but no current actionable proposal; S3 has a feasible v2 but lacks D's current endorsement; S4 permits v2. This is a reviewer recheck of the reference logic, not a second independently implemented scorer or a software integration test. Research source and result files were unchanged.

The integrated documents accurately describe the prototype as unimplemented for research purposes, the key check as synthetic, the task bank as unfinished, the study as unregistered, and the deadline as unknown. They do not claim full-book access, empirical efficacy, deployed-code behavior or human expert endorsement. Final packaging should preserve these precise distinctions. Completing this research design does not require completing its future studies.
