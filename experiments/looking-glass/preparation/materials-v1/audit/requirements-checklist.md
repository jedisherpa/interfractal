## Acceptance checklist for the first materials packet

1. **Finite corpus and allocation.** There must be exactly 18 unique, versioned synthetic cards: two identical shared anchor cards plus four distinct non-anchor cards for each of four future agent packets. Each packet therefore has six cards, and the 24 packet placements resolve to 18 unique card IDs. Both anchors appear unchanged in all four packets. Repetition of an anchor is one source, not four independent corroborations. Freeze stable opaque IDs, exact content hashes, allocation, source relations, and one common cutoff; document any later revisions rather than overwriting originals. Cover exploration, understanding, explicit agreement, changed requirements, and unresolved difference. [07, First experiment; 01, Working vocabulary; current ledger, Eighteen-card corpus]
2. **Typed semantics.** Every claimed relation must identify predicate, argument roles and direction where meaningful, supporting and conflicting source IDs, cutoff/version, scope, alternatives, and unknowns. Similar vocabulary or geometry is not evidence of the same relationship. Keep a claimed interpretation, understanding of a proposal, endorsement, commitment of resources, task outcome, and delegated authority as different types. A model's attribution cannot create a person's attestation or action right. Unknown, withheld, not asked, disputed, declined, and withdrawn are distinct. [07, Visualizer contract; 01, vocabulary; 04, Canonical data contract]
3. **Task bank and blind key.** Development and withheld tasks must be clearly separated. All scored tasks need a versioned prompt, allowed input card IDs/cutoff, requested answer type, scoring rule, source-linked expected answer, acceptable equivalent formulations, and a defined insufficient-evidence response when facts do not entail a unique answer. Cover relational transfer, misleading-analogy rejection, consequential-distinction recovery, and coordination under a changed requirement. Include similar appearance with different relation; different appearance with same relation; correct understanding paired with explicit rejection; and a new constraint that invalidates an earlier summary. Do not tune on withheld answers. [07, First experiment; 03, P1/P2; current ledger, Withheld tasks]
4. **Consequence and action scope.** A difference counts as consequential only relative to a specified inference, action, obligation, limit or correction. For each action-like answer, specify physical/resource feasibility separately from mandate/consent and from information sufficiency. A later card must state whether it supersedes, narrows, or merely adds evidence to an earlier card. Do not infer commitment from mere compatibility or a fluent shared summary. [07, constructs and unfamiliar tests; 01, vocabulary; 03, P1]
5. **Snapshot and prompt package.** Prepare, but do not execute, four isolated initial packet prompts with the same perspective instruction unless a separate factor is explicitly varied. A snapshot schema must include actor/model/config identity, exact input IDs and versions, common cutoff, relation roles, source links, conflicting evidence, alternatives/unknowns, confidence provenance, possible actions, distinction between understanding and commitment, and revision lineage. Freeze model/version, settings, token/tool budgets, isolation, bounded exchange, and exact packet/input hashes before any later research run. Engineering workers and their messages are not the four research participants or outputs. [07, First experiment; 02, Honest status and execution rules; current ledger, Snapshot package]
6. **Future comparison plan.** Prespecify simple pooling, an adequately provisioned full-union solver, and bounded interactive synthesis with information access and total generation/retrieval/synthesis cost charged. Shared anchor repetitions must be deduplicated. Preserve independent initial snapshots before discussion, log revisions and lost dissent, and plan independent repetitions/allocations; one quartet is only a demonstration. No comparator or four-agent outcome is claimed by a materials-preparation check. [07, Comparisons and inference; 03, P0/P1; current ledger, Bench comparators]
7. **Plain/visual equivalence and inspection.** A future plain and visual instrument must expose the same substantive evidence and operations: exact source/version inspection, typed relations, correction/dissent, retained originals/revisions, export, keyboard path and plain fallback. Map every visual element to a declared operational meaning or label it layout only. A camera/layout change, ID relabeling, or repeated source must not change the answer, rights or permission state. Same information does not imply equal human effort or proven visual benefit. [07, Visualizer contract; 03, P1/P3; 04, views and acceptance corpus]
8. **Preparation boundary and disposition.** The materials packet should be independently reviewed before a future research launch and list every unresolved ambiguity with its scoring consequence and owner. Relevant software acceptance remains separate: no unresolved consequential source loss, false attestation, stale-version action or rendering-induced permission change in the declared corpus. Gate 10's historical blocked workflow cannot be relabeled ready. Human protocol/readiness and an updated verified preparation ZIP are still required for the larger goal, but actual four-agent or human collection is deferred. Gate 14 is unapproved and independent of this materials review. [03, P1/readiness; 04, acceptance corpus; current ledger]

## Consequential ambiguities to resolve before a scored key

- **Evidence universe and time.** Declare whether each task is answered from one packet, the union, a bounded exchange, or a later revision, and exactly which cutoff/version applies. Otherwise a correct “unknown” for one view can be incorrectly scored against a union-only fact.
- **Authority and commitment.** Define who may authorize or commit each action and what exact record constitutes endorsement. A statement of understanding, a generated suggestion, and a fictional actor's stipulated agreement must not be silently collapsed.
- **Duplicate anchors and source dependence.** Specify source identity independent of packet placement. Four sightings of the same anchor are one source; related revisions/windows are not fresh corroboration.
- **Relation identity.** Specify the predicate and ordered argument roles, including whether a transfer relation survives changed surface features. Similar words or visual proximity alone are insufficient.
- **Changed requirements and supersession.** State temporal ordering, effective version, which earlier conclusions become stale, and whether the new requirement changes feasibility, authority, or both.
- **Insufficient evidence.** Identify what missing fact would decide each underdetermined task and permit abstention. An apparently natural answer must not be forced when multiple worlds satisfy the supplied cards.
- **Scoring and held-out contamination.** Define partial credit, source citation requirements, ties/alternative answers, false certainty penalty, and withheld-task access boundary before later outputs. Any task whose wording reveals its answer should be revised before freeze.
- **Representational parity.** Define the exact information and operations held constant across plain and visual materials; navigation cost may differ and must not be hidden by a post hoc exclusion.

## Blind derivation protocol

When root provides the **public card, allocation, task and rule paths only**, I will read only those files, pin their bytes, and save a source-ID-linked answer derivation in this audit directory. For every task I will enumerate the entailed answer or explicit underdetermination, a minimal evidence chain, counterexample where a tempting analogy fails, and any ambiguity that changes scoring. Root should freeze that derivation before allowing comparison with the author answer key, authored expected answers, scoring outputs, or solver. Any later comparison will be a separate, explicitly labeled phase; it cannot rewrite the blind result.
