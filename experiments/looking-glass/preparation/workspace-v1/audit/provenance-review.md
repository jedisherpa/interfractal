## Key trace limits that must not become scoring errors

1. **Seventy-three transfer atoms lack per-atom canonical evidence.** W01–W05 omit it for `selection`, `linEndorsesV1` and `utterancePredicate` (15 atoms). W06 provides one flat evidence list per context rather than per bundle/field (58 atoms). The missing trace does not invalidate the value, but a provenance scorer must derive a field-specific certificate from source and rules. The W06 flat list cannot tell whether a particular bundle used one bay, two distinct bays, or no resource.
2. **Four transfer unknowns carry partial nonempty author evidence.** W02/W03 `commitment` and `readiness` are `unknown`, yet their key arrays cite present grants, capacities or rules. Those citations establish subcomponents, not the absence of the required exact grant. The public development provenance convention says an unknown atom has evidence `[]` plus a named missing dependency. For W02 name `LIN/bench Q7@1 quantity 3`; for W03 name both `LIN/bench Q7@2 quantity 3` and `NOA/courier Q7@2 quantity 1`. A scorer should preserve partial evidence at component level while requiring those gaps for the top-level unknown. Do not falsely mark the values refuted or silently accept a partial citation as complete proof.
3. **Development key evidence does not include missing-dependency fields.** This matters for 80 `unknown` statuses, 17 null dependent/scalar fields and four packet `none_supported` choices. For example A1's D08 choice lists actual press facts and rules, but it needs the missing cart/other positive dependencies to explain why no plan is supported-ready. A correct shorter proof with named gaps deserves credit even if its citation set differs from the key's broad list. A4 P10 readiness is refuted by the negative inspection path even while physical and commitment are unknown; requiring all components to be known would be wrong.
4. **Catalog fields are proof premises outside claim IDs.** D07 demand thresholds and D08 output/cost use the supplied plan catalog; W06 output/cost require the selected X/Y catalog entries. The key's claim/rule arrays alone cannot express those catalog field paths, so a source-aware scorer must include exact catalog context/version as a premise or a typed catalog reference. It cannot award provenance for `output=12` merely because `R08` or an unrelated grant is cited. This is a scoring-contract completion need, not a discovered wrong numeric answer.
5. **Some valid shorter proofs omit key extras.** For union P8 readiness, `F-C17-1` plus plan output8 and R02/R07 suffices; the key additionally cites capacities/grants. For union P10 readiness, corrected varnish0 or Bea's exact cart refusal gives a refutation, though the key also lists the conflict reports and other components. For W05, in contrast, citing only `W05-F7` cannot prove `conflicted`: `W05-F8` is necessary to establish the negative side. Exact array equality to the author key is therefore neither necessary nor sufficient for provenance credit.

Counterexample probes for the future synthetic scorer should reject: a P12 physical `supported` claim citing only Ara's press capacity while Bea's cart capacity is unavailable; `F-C11-1` as current varnish after C12 supersedes it; `F-C16-1` promoted from model attribution to Ara's endorsement; W03 version-1 grants used for catalog Q7@2; W05 `supported` commitment from only its positive grant while its exact negative grant is present; W06-SHARED X+Y treated as two physical bay units because it has two conditional plan grants; and an `unknown` with only a present partial claim but no named missing exact dependency. These use synthetic authored fixtures only, never actual research responses.

The existing key and prior blind value agreement should remain frozen. The appropriate implementation disposition is a separate, explicit provenance evaluator with scoped active-source lookup, exact directed-role/version matching, four-valued compositional rules, catalog premises, named missing dependencies, and reviewer-visible reason codes. Value scoring and proof scoring must remain separate, as `SCORING_AND_ANALYSIS.md` requires. Where a proof is genuinely ambiguous, retain disagreement and consider a new reviewed materials revision; do not edit the accepted key or silently rescore outcomes.
