## What the primary studies establish for design

### 1. Yoking the watched stream is the relevant control

Harman, Humphrey, and Goodale (1999) compared active manual rotation of novel 3-D objects with passive viewing of the exact same image sequence. Active viewers later recognized objects more efficiently. The study is a direct precedent for replaying an actor's realized sequence to a passive viewer, rather than giving the passive condition an independently chosen or generic sequence ([original article record and abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10574764/); [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0960-9822(00)80053-6)).

For Gate 9, the watch route should receive the chooser's exact baseline plus the two realized `project`/`xw90`/`yv90` query occurrences, in the same order, including repeats. Preserve source-card IDs, query labels, occurrence indices, and the terminal information hash. A software route check can establish that the records match; it cannot establish that passive viewers remember them or that choice improves performance. Harman's object-rotation task also leaves action-related cues, motor involvement, and object-specific recognition as possible explanations, so it does not identify an agency mechanism for the finite 5-D worlds.

### 2. “Active versus passive” bundles several timing and attention differences

Markant, DuBrow, Davachi, and Gureckis (2014) ran four experiments that separated control over study content from control over timing. Self-directed study improved recognition relative to passive/yoked study even when control was reduced to pressing a button when ready for the next item ([open manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4281964/); [publisher record and DOI](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-014-0435-9)). The authors' central methodological point is that a yoked sequence can match content while still mismatching the learner's attentional readiness, episode onset, duration, revisitation pattern, and other exposure details.

Gate 9 should therefore freeze the exposure contract before any human use: identical practice, baseline, exactly two query occurrences, query order, duplicate handling, per-card display duration or viewing window, stopping rule, and terminal submission window. Practice should be the same across conditions and excluded from scored exposure. The chooser's adaptive route is the treatment record; the watch condition is yoked to that record, not to a precomputed “typical” route. The static condition should retain every duplicate and the same card order while declaring its extra reinspection and simultaneous-access affordance as part of the condition. Markant's outcome is recognition memory in a spatial object task; it does not validate the Gate 9 point-world model or show that any condition is superior here.

### 3. Static and temporal displays can differ even when task content is named the same

Rolfes, Roth, and Schnotz (2020) randomly assigned 157 secondary-school students to static, linear dynamic, or interactive dynamic visualizations with the same mathematical task content. The dynamic groups outperformed the static group, while linear and interactive dynamic groups did not differ. The paper describes a key design asymmetry: static learners could revisit a representation but had to mentally integrate change, whereas a linear dynamic display exposed temporal change but was transient ([open primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7212367/); [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00693)).

This is a caution for Gate 9, not a predicted result. “Information matched at terminal” does not mean the three routes have matched temporal access, memory load, or pixels. The static sheet's all-card access, the watch route's ordered transient exposure, and the chooser's agency are intended condition differences. Instrument validation should compare the frozen records and terminal exact hash; any later human outcome must be labeled as a new observation with uncertainty. The study used a 25-minute mathematics lesson, an immediate posttest, and no actor-to-observer yoking, so its learning result cannot be transferred to the finite 5-D card task.

## Gate 9 source-grounded requirements

1. Freeze four finite 5-D point worlds and one fixed reference. Record the baseline observation and exactly two query occurrences from the declared `project`/`xw90`/`yv90` menu. Repeats remain records, not silently deduplicated observations.
2. Let the chooser produce the realized query order. The watch route replays that exact ordered occurrence stream. A watch route with substituted or independently optimized queries is not yoked.
3. Keep the static route's cards identical to the chooser/watch records, including duplicates and order. Record that simultaneous access and reinspection are condition affordances; they are not evidence of extra raw source information.
4. Match practice, exposure budget, stopping, submission, and terminal information. Check route equality mechanically before any interpretation. Keep timing, memory, agency, and pixel differences explicit rather than treating terminal equality as full parity.
5. Software checks may establish route/data parity and reproducibility only. No participant, learning, memory, agency, causal, or display-advantage claim is authorized by this source pass.

## Limits

The papers are primary human experiments, but they study object recognition, episodic memory, and mathematics learning rather than this instrument. Their effects cannot be pooled into a Gate 9 estimate. They support control and measurement choices, not a prediction about which route will perform better. In particular, exact raw-record equality does not imply equal visual pixels, equal attentional opportunity, equal time-on-card, equal memory trace, or equal motor/action experience.

Host scope clarification before freeze: the final menu includes `project` as well as `xw90` and `yv90`. No terminal submission or scored practice exists in this software gate; those source-informed human-protocol suggestions remain future considerations. Actual timing and exposure are not matched across the three routes.
