03 / DECLARED AMBIGUITY

Two source balls, one initial view

Candidate class: unit 4D balls centered at w=+0.5 or w=−0.5. Both have the same xyz projection and the same w=0 slice. A known w=+0.5 slice separates these two candidates.

TWO DECLARED CANDIDATES

A camera change cannot distinguish the initial raw 3D sets. The intervention distinguishes only this declared pair; it does not reconstruct an arbitrary 4D shape.

A stored 3D animation makes the same movie

What to watch

At 0s the slice is the full unit ball; at 16s it is a single location; at 20s it is empty, while the projection still fills a unit ball. At 24s the point returns. In the ambiguity card, w=0 agrees and w=+0.5 separates the two declared candidates.

The saved 3D animation matches the selected 401 projected movie frames. A known −90° source turn at 20s separates this fixed stored animation from the analytic source. Movie appearance alone cannot establish a 4D cause.
